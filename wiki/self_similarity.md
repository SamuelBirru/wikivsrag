# Self-similarity

## Overview
This paper introduces self-similar solutions to the vorticity dynamics, which describe the evolution of the norm |Wg|∞ as a function of time. The solutions are found for different ranges of g, including g > 1/2 (first-kind self-similarity), -1/2 < g < 1/2 (second-kind self-similarity), and g < -1/2 (third-kind self-similarity). These solutions describe the asymptotic behavior of the vorticity norm as time approaches infinity.
