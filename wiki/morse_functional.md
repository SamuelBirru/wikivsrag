# Morse Functional

## Overview
The authors construct a gauge fixing that yields an on-shell Gribov operator containing a positive mass term proportional to ν2β, solved by the Morse functional in a theory with spontaneous symmetry breaking.

- [Non-abelian field cohomology, its relation with spontaneous symmetry breaking and Morse's Theorem](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06159v1)
