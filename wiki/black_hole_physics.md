# Black Hole Physics

## Overview

Black hole physics is the study of the properties and behavior of black holes, regions of spacetime where gravity is so strong that not even light can escape. The intense gravitational pull of these objects warps the fabric of spacetime around them, creating strange effects such as gravitational waves and frame-dragging. Understanding black holes is crucial to our understanding of the universe, from the behavior of matter and energy under extreme conditions to the nature of spacetime itself.

## Key Findings from Literature

• **Effects of dark matter on rotating black hole spacetimes**: Research has shown that dark matter can significantly impact the behavior and appearance of rotating black holes. This includes the formation of photon rings and the creation of adaptive ray tracing signatures.
• **Trace anomaly and effective approach**: The study of trace anomalies and effective approaches in black hole physics has revealed new insights into the properties of these objects. These findings have significant implications for our understanding of gravity and the behavior of matter under extreme conditions.
• **Gravitational potential**: Understanding the gravitational potential of black holes is crucial to understanding their behavior and effects on spacetime. Research has shown that the gravitational potential of black holes can be described using an effective approach, which provides a more intuitive understanding of these objects.
