# Galois Groups

## Overview
A mathematical concept used to determine the solvability of polynomial equations. In this paper, it is used to analyze the irreducible polynomials that govern the Bethe roots and their unsolvability for chains of eight or more sites.

## Papers
- [Galois Solvability of Finite-Size Bethe Solutions in the Heisenberg Chain](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05589v1)
