# Heavy-ion collision experiments

## Overview
Heavy-ion collision experiments aim to study the properties of the quark-gluon plasma created in these collisions, including the effects of in-medium mass modifications and squeezing back-to-back correlations. The RHIC (Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider) at Brookhaven National Laboratory is one such experiment.
