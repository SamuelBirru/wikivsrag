# Quantum Generative Adversarial Networks (QGANs)

## Overview
QGANs are a type of generative adversarial networks that use quantum computing to generate new data. In this paper, QGANs are implemented on a quantum neural network chip, demonstrating its capabilities in generating high-fidelity images.
