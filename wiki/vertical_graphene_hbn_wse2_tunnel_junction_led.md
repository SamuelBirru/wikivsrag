# Vertical Graphene/hBN/WSe2 Tunnel-Junction LED

## Overview
Demonstrates the fabrication and operation of an atomically thin quantum light-emitting diode (QLED) based on a vertical graphene/hBN/WSe2 heterostructure, showing electrical excitation of monolayer WSe2 with localized emission near threshold.
