# Matter-Radiation Equality

## Overview
The paper explores the concept of matter-radiation equality, which is a critical point in the evolution of the universe where the matter density and radiation density are equal. The authors study the effects of an accelerating expansion on this point and discuss its implications for our understanding of the early universe.
