# Generalized Eigenvalue Problem

## Overview
The paper provides a quantum-assisted variational algorithm for solving the generalized eigenvalue problem A|ψ⟩ = λB|ψ⟩ for arbitrary non-Hermitian complex matrices A and B by reducing it to a matrix triangularization task via generalized Schur decomposition. It establishes quantitative relationships between the accuracy of the computed generalized eigenvalues ε, the accuracy of the loss function εL, and the precision σ of encoding optimization parameters, finding εL ~ ε² and ε^(min) ~ √σ. These scaling relations are confirmed numerically on ensembles of random 4×4 complex matrices.
