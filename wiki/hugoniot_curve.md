# Hugoniot curve

## Overview
The Hugoniot curve is a mathematical surface that describes the boundary between the pre-shock and post-shock regions of a shock wave. In this paper, we derive the Hugoniot curve for vanishing viscosity shock waves using the polytropic gas law.
