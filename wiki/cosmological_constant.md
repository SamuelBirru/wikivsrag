# Cosmological Constant

## Overview
The paper discusses the possibility of a cosmological constant, which is a measure of the energy density of the vacuum. The authors explore the effects of a non-zero cosmological constant on the evolution of the universe and discuss its implications for our understanding of dark energy.

- [Cosmological Dynamics of a Non-Canonical Generalised Brans-Dicke Theory](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06147v1)
