# Chirality-induced spin selectivity

## Overview
This paper explores the phenomenon of chirality-induced spin selectivity, which refers to the selective interaction between electrons and chiral molecules. The authors develop a theoretical framework for understanding this effect, including the role of exchange interactions and the impact of environmental factors on the spin polarization of individual molecules. This research has significant implications for the development of quantum computing and spintronics technologies.
