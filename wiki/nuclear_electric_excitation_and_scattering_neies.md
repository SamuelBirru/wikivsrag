# Nuclear Electric Excitation and Scattering (NEIES)

## Overview
This paper introduces Nuclear Electric Excitation and Scattering (NEIES) as a process that excites a nucleus through the interaction with an incoming electron. The authors discuss the selection rules, angular momentum quantum numbers, and energy dependence of NEIES cross sections for various nuclear transitions.
