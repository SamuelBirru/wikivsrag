# In-medium mass modification

## Overview
An in-medium mass modification refers to a change in the mass of particles within the hot and dense matter created in relativistic heavy-ion collisions, which can lead to various effects such as squeezing back-to-back correlations and changes in single-particle invariant momentum distributions.
