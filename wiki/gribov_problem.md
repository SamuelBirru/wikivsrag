# Gribov Problem

## Overview
The paper shows that in a gauge theory with spontaneous symmetry breaking, the on-shell Gribov operator acquires a positive mass term proportional to the symmetry breaking scale squared, lifting the zero modes responsible for Gribov copies. This means that for perturbative energies below the symmetry breaking scale, the Gribov problem is automatically resolved on-shell without requiring explicit non-local modifications such as the Gribov-Zwanziger action. The result holds for any gauge fixing constructed from a Morse functional of ultraviolet dimension 2, including 't Hooft-type gauges.
