# Ricci-Gauss-Bonnet Theories

## Overview
The Ricci-Gauss-Bonnet theories are a class of gravitational theories that describe the behavior of spacetime in terms of curvature and topology. This paper applies these theories to study dark energy and its relation to Hoêva-Lifshitz gravity.
