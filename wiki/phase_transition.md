# Phase Transition

## Overview
Phase transition is a fundamental concept in physics that describes the change from one state to another, often characterized by a critical point where the system undergoes a sudden change in behavior. This concept has been extensively studied in various fields of physics, including statistical mechanics and condensed matter physics. Understanding phase transitions is crucial for understanding complex systems and predicting their behavior.

## Key Findings from Literature
* The Potts machine model exhibits a phase transition as hyperparameters are varied, revealing a critical point at which the system undergoes a drastic change in behavior (Source: Comparative Study of Potts Machine Dynamics and Performance for Max-k-Cut).
* Phase transitions are characterized by a sudden change in the behavior of a system, often accompanied by a critical point where the system's properties exhibit non-analytic behavior (Source: Lecture Notes on Statistical Physics and Neural Networks).
* The study of phase transitions has implications for understanding complex systems, including neural networks and machine learning algorithms.

## Related Concepts
* Critical Point: A point in space where the system undergoes a sudden change in behavior.
* Non-equilibrium Phase Transitions: Transitions that occur away from equilibrium, often exhibiting unique properties.
* Order Parameter: A quantity used to describe the order of a phase transition.
* Universality Classes: A set of behaviors exhibited by systems undergoing similar phase transitions.
