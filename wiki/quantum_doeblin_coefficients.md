# Quantum Doeblin Coefficients

## Overview
The authors introduce a new concept, quantum Doeblin coefficients, which provide a measure of the contractivity of quantum channels under different divergence measures, with implications for understanding quantum information processing and thermodynamics.

## Papers
- [Tight Contraction Rates for Primitive Channels under Quantum $f$-Divergences](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06452v1)
