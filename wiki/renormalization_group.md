# Renormalization Group

## Overview
The paper presents the renormalization group as a procedure of marginalizing probability distributions by summing over (integrating out) subsets of degrees of freedom, worked out explicitly for both the 1D and 2D Ising models. It derives scaling laws and critical exponents directly from the existence of non-trivial RG fixed points with relevant perturbations, making the connection between fixed-point stability and second-order phase transitions concrete and accessible. Crucially, it draws a formal analogy between this RG integration procedure and the way hidden neurons are integrated out in restricted Boltzmann machines, providing a unifying conceptual bridge between statistical physics and machine learning.
