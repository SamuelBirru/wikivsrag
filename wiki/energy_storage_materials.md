# Energy Storage Materials

## Overview
This research contributes to the understanding of energy storage materials by investigating the isomer depletion via NEIES. The study of isomer deple- tion mechanisms provides valuable insights into the long-term stability and performance of these materials, which are crucial for various applications in fields such as medicine, industry, and nuclear energy.
