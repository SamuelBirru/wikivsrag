# Moyal Residual

## Overview
The Moyal residual is a correction term that arises in the evolution of the Wigner function, which represents the difference between the exact dynamics and the Fokker-Planck closure. This paper studies the properties and importance of the Moyal residual in understanding dynamical nonclassicality.
