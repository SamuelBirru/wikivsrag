# Gravitational Field Equations

## Overview
The gravitational field equations are a set of nonlinear partial differential equations that describe the motion of mass and energy in curved spacetime. This paper uses the YTSF integrable structure to study the solutions to these equations, specifically in the context of Tomimatsu-Sato geometry.
