# Exchange interactions

## Overview
This paper develops a theoretical framework for understanding exchange interactions in chiral molecules, including the role of orbital hybridization and environmental factors. The authors propose a new approach to calculating exchange energies using a combination of density functional theory and Monte Carlo simulations. This research advances our understanding of the fundamental principles underlying spintronics technologies.
