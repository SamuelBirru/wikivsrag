# Momentum Sum Rule

## Overview
The momentum sum rule is proven by evaluating the model expression for the unpolarized TMD and showing that it equals N_q A_2 M Z d3k h t^2 0 + √k^2t^2 1 i. This result is derived from the equation of motion in coordinate space and the conservation of the energy-momentum tensor.
