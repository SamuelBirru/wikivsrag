# Non-intersection property

## Overview
A crucial property used to derive the singular log-interacting SDE for the dynamics of GLN(C) Brownian motion, obtained from certain Lyapunov functions.

## Papers
- [$\mathsf{GL}_N(\mathbb{C})$ Brownian motion and stochastic PDE on entire functions](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06429v1)
