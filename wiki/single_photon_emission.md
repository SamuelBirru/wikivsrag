# Single-Photon Emission

## Overview
The paper reports the first observation of fully integrated single-photon emission from ion-implanted Er3+ defects in a 4H-SiCOI photonic platform at room temperature, confirmed by a photon autocorrelation value of g(2)(0) = 0.04 without background correction. This result is achieved using a single-detector photon correlation scheme enabled by the long Purcell-reduced lifetime relative to detector dead time. The work establishes that room-temperature single-photon emission in the telecom C-band is achievable without cryogenic cooling in this material system.
