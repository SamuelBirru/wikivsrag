# Bethe Ansatz

## Overview
A mathematical approach used to solve exactly solvable quantum many-body systems, including the Heisenberg chain. This paper extends the known symbolic ground states of the Heisenberg chain to up to the ten-site case, revealing that finite-size Bethe solutions can rapidly develop unsolvable algebraic complexity despite the integrability of the underlying model.
