# BRST Cohomology

## Overview
The paper demonstrates that spontaneous symmetry breaking changes the cohomological structure of a non-abelian gauge theory by creating new field combinations that behave like matter fields rather than gauge fields under the BRST operator. Using the filtration theorem, the filtered BRST operator after symmetry breaking is shown to leave specific combinations of gauge and scalar fields invariant, which can then be lifted to full BRST-invariant observables. This cohomological reclassification is the central mechanism that enables the resolution of the Gribov problem.
