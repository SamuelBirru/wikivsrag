# Majorana Field

## Overview
The Majorana field is a type of fermionic field that has been used to study the properties of quantum systems, including the Bell-CHSH inequality. In this paper, we derive a rapidity-space formula for the vacuum Bell-CHSH correlator using the Bisognano-Wichmann theorem and modular wedge localization for the massive Majorana field in 1+1 dimensions.
