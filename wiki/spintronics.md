# Spintronics

## Overview
This paper highlights the importance of spintronics in the development of new technologies, including quantum computing and spin-based devices. The authors explore the potential for chirality-induced spin selectivity to be used in spintronics applications, such as spin transfer torque and magnetoresistance. This research has significant implications for the advancement of spintronics technologies.
