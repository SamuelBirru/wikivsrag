# Quantum Simulation

## Overview

Quantum simulation is a technique used in physics to study complex quantum systems by simulating their behavior on a quantum computer. This allows researchers to investigate phenomena that are difficult or impossible to study using classical computers, enabling new insights into the behavior of matter and energy at the quantum level. By leveraging the capabilities of quantum computers, scientists can gain a deeper understanding of quantum mechanics and its applications.

## Key Findings from Literature

* Quantum simulation enables the study of complex quantum systems that are difficult or impossible to investigate using classical computers.
* The technique has been successfully applied to simulate various physical phenomena, including string-breaking dynamics in gauge theories.
* Quantum simulation allows for the exploration of non-equilibrium dynamics and out-of-equilibrium processes.
