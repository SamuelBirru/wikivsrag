# Bell-CHSH Inequality

## Overview
The Bell-CHSH inequality is a fundamental concept in quantum mechanics that describes the limits of local realism. This paper studies the violation of the Bell-CHSH inequality for the massive Majorana field in 1+1 dimensions using modular wedge localization.
