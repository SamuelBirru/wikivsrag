# Soliton Equations

## Overview
Soliton equations are a type of nonlinear partial differential equation that describe the propagation of solitons, stable waves with finite energy. This paper uses soliton theory as a background to study the YTSF integrable structure in the context of Tomimatsu-Sato geometry.
