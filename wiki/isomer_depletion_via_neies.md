# Isomer Depletion via NEIES

## Overview
This paper explores the process of isomer depletion via Nuclear Electric Excitation and Scattering (NEIES), which involves the excitation of a nucleus at an isomeric state through NEIES and subsequent decay to the ground state. The authors investigate the NEIES cross sections for various isotopes and their effects on isomer depletion. This work has significant implications for energy storage materials, as it provides insight into the mechanisms underlying the depleting of nuclear isomers.
