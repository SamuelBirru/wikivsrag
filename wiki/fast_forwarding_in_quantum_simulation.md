# Fast-Forwarding in Quantum Simulation

## Overview
Experimental demonstration of fast-forwarding for near-term quantum hardware, showcasing the feasibility of compressed Trotter terms for simulating nonadiabatic dynamics with reduced resource requirements.

## Papers
- [Variationally Compressing Quantum Circuits to Approximate Nonadiabatic Molecular Quantum Dynamics](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06122v1)
