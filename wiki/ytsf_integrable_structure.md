# YTSF Integrable Structure

## Overview
The YTSF (Yang-Tau-Soliton Functional) integrable structure is a mathematical framework for studying nonlinear evolution equations. This paper demonstrates its application to the stationary axisymmetric Einstein field equations, providing an example of its realization in Tomimatsu-Sato geometry.
