# Squeezing effect in heavy-ion collisions

## Overview
The squeezing effect is a phenomenon observed in relativistic heavy-ion collisions where particle interactions within the medium induce an in-medium mass modification, leading to squeezed back-to-back correlations of particle-antiparticle pairs.
