# Fluctuation Relations

## Overview
This paper introduces a new type of fluctuation relation that compares forward and reversed weighted path contributions, rather than just positive probabilities. This relation provides insights into the nature of quantum dynamics and can be used to diagnose dynamical nonclassicality.
