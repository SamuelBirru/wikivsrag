# Dissipative Jaynes-Cummings Model

## Overview
The paper applies the single-excitation sector of the Lindblad-governed Jaynes-Cummings model as a minimal platform to exhibit non-trivial non-equilibrium relaxation phenomena, fully characterizing the Liouvillian eigenspectrum including an exceptional point at a critical cavity decay rate. It shows that the spectral separation between slow (Purcell-enhanced atomic decay at rate ~4g²/κ) and fast (cavity relaxation at rate ~κ) eigenmodes is the key structural feature enabling the Pontus-Mpemba effect.
