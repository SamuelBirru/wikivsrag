# Ginsparg-Wilson Relation

## Overview
The paper demonstrates that a neural network trained with the GW relation as a soft loss constraint can reproduce the overlap Dirac operator to high numerical accuracy in both 2D and 4D without any prescribed polynomial or rational approximation. More strikingly, within a generalized polynomial ansatz, the network autonomously recovers the standard GW relation (c1=1, cn>1=0) from an agnostic initialization, and by shifting the initial search bias discovers a distinct Fujikawa-type generalized GW relation. This establishes machine-learning-based algebraic discovery as a new route to exploring the landscape of chiral lattice fermion structures.
