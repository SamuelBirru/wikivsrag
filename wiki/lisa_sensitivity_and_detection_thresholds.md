# LISA Sensitivity and Detection Thresholds

## Overview
This paper evaluates the sensitivity of LISA to detect stochastic signals from eccentric sBBHs and assesses the impact of Galactic foreground noise on signal detection.

## Papers
- [Implications of the LISA stochastic signal from eccentric stellar mass black hole binaries in vacuum](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05537v1)
