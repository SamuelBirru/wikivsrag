# Quantum computing

## Overview
This paper demonstrates the potential for chirality-induced spin selectivity to be used in quantum computing applications, such as quantum error correction and quantum teleportation. The authors propose a novel approach to harnessing this effect using chiral molecules as quantum bits. This research advances our understanding of the fundamental principles underlying quantum computation.
