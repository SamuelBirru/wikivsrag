# Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking

## Overview
The paper establishes a precise link between spontaneous symmetry breaking and the cohomological properties of field combinations, showing that the broken vacuum fundamentally alters which field combinations are BRST-closed. In the SU(2) x U(1) example broken to U(1)_em, specific linear combinations of gauge and Higgs fields are identified as cohomologically matter-like, which is the property that ultimately drives the on-shell mass generation in the Gribov operator. This connects the symmetry breaking scale directly to the elimination of gauge copy ambiguities.
