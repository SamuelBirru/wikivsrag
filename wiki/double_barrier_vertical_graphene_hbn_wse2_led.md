# Double-Barrier Vertical Graphene/hBN/WSe2 LED

## Overview
Explores the effect of thin hBN tunnel barriers on carrier injection and defect emission in a vertical graphene/hBN/WSe2 heterostructure, demonstrating the potential for narrowband, antibunched emission from localized defects.
