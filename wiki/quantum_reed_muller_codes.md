# Quantum Reed-Muller Codes

## Overview
The paper reveals the algebraic product structure underlying the 'unfolding' of quantum Reed-Muller codes, generalizing a previously SAT-solver-discovered 2D layout to a systematic framework applicable to larger codes. It constructs explicit 2D planar layouts for QRM4(1,1) and QRM6(1,1) and 3D local layouts for QRM6(1,2) and QRM7(2,2) by partitioning coordinate indices into groups corresponding to spatial directions and exploiting Gray-code-ordered bases.
