# Filippov differential inclusion

## Overview
Provides a rigorous framework for modeling the forced vibro-impact oscillator, including the Filippov set-valued vector field F and its associated inequalities.

## Papers
- [Mixed Global Dynamics of the Forced Vibro-Impact Oscillator with Coulomb Friction and its Symplectic Structure, KAM Tori, and Persistence](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05605v1)
