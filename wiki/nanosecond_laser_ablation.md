# Nanosecond Laser Ablation

## Overview
The paper investigates the use of nanosecond laser ablation for cleaning microfabricated ion traps, including the ablation thresholds for various materials and the effectiveness of this technique for removing contamination.

- [Ablation Removal of Transport-Blocking Defects in Surface-Electrode Ion Traps](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06312v1)
