# Scalar-Tensor Theory

## Overview
The paper develops a scalar-tensor theory of gravity, which is an alternative to general relativity. The theory includes a scalar field that interacts with matter and radiation, and the paper studies its effects on various cosmological phenomena, such as the evolution of the universe and the formation of structure.
