# Accelerating Expansion

## Overview
The paper explores the possibility of an accelerating expansion of the universe due to a scalar field, which is a deviation from the standard model of gravity. The effects of this acceleration are studied on various cosmological scales, including the evolution of the matter-radiation equality. The results show that the acceleration can be significant for certain values of the coupling parameter, and the paper discusses the implications of these findings.
