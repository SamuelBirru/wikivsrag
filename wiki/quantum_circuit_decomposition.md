# Quantum Circuit Decomposition

## Overview
The paper derives a universal, shortest analytic quantum circuit decomposition for arbitrary diagonal unitary matrices of any size, achieving algorithmic complexity of O(2^n) in the number of CNOT gates, compared to O(2.5^n) for prior numerical methods. It introduces two recursive circuit architectures—a 'Strange Fractal' and a 'Binary Tree'—for constructing the CNOT gate sequence, and proves that the mapping between diagonal matrix phase parameters and circuit rotation parameters is strictly linear.
