# Non-Perturbative Effects in AdS/CFT

## Overview
This paper explores the non-perturbative corrections to the spectrum of bosonic and fermionic states in the context of AdS/CFT correspondence. It shows that these corrections are not only significant but also distinct between different types of states, indicating a breakdown of the expected degeneracy at large twist angles.
