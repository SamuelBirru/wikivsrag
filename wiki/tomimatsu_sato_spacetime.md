# Tomimatsu-Sato Spacetime

## Overview
The Tomimatsu-Sato spacetime is a type of axially symmetric black hole solution in general relativity, first introduced by Akio Tomimatsu and Hiroshi Sato in 1972. This paper provides an example of the YTSF integrable structure in the context of Tomimatsu-Sato geometry.
