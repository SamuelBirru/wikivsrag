# Bisognano-Wichmann Theorem

## Overview
The Bisognano-Wichmann theorem is a fundamental result in quantum field theory that describes the properties of the vacuum state. In this paper, we use the Bisognano-Wichmann theorem and modular wedge localization to derive a rapidity-space formula for the vacuum Bell-CHSH correlator.
