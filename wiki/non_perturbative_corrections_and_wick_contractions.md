# Non-Perturbative Corrections and Wick Contractions

## Overview
The paper investigates how non-perturbative corrections arise from Wick contractions of fundamental fields, leading to significant differences between bosonic and fermionic states. These differences persist even at large twist angles, indicating a breakdown of the expected degeneracy.
