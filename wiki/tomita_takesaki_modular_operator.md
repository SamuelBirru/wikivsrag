# Tomita-Takesaki Modular Operator

## Overview
The Tomita-Takesaki modular operator is a fundamental concept in C*-algebras and has been used to study the properties of quantum systems, including the Bell-CHSH inequality. This paper derives a rapidity-space formula for the vacuum Bell-CHSH correlator using the Bisognano-Wichmann theorem and modular wedge localization. The result shows that the value is below 2√2 with the Tsirelson value being the supremum reached by spectral concentration near ω=0 or equivalently near λ^2(ω)=1.
