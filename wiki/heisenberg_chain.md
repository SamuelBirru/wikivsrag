# Heisenberg Chain

## Overview
A one-dimensional quantum many-body system with antiferromagnetic interactions. This paper presents explicit symbolic ground states for this model up to the ten-site case, providing insight into the behavior of finite-size Bethe solutions and their limitations.

## Papers
- [Galois Solvability of Finite-Size Bethe Solutions in the Heisenberg Chain](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05589v1)
