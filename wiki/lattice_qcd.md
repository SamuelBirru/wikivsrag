# Lattice QCD

## Overview
The authors use Lattice QCD with Domain Wall Fermions to simulate chiral fermions on the lattice, allowing for precise calculations of physical quantities such as quark masses and couplings.

## Papers
- [$F_K/F_π$ as a precision test of a new four flavor Domain Wall Fermion action](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06560v1)
