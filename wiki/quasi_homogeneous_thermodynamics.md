# Quasi-Homogeneous Thermodynamics

## Overview
The paper formally establishes that the fundamental equations of Reissner-Nordström, Kerr, and Kerr-Newman black holes are quasi-homogeneous functions with specific scaling weights, rather than the standard degree-one homogeneous functions of laboratory thermodynamics. It derives a generalized Euler identity and Gibbs-Duhem relation appropriate for these systems, resolving the inconsistency that arises when standard extensivity is naively applied to self-gravitating systems. This quasi-homogeneity is shown to be a mandatory physical requirement, not merely a mathematical convenience, for building a consistent thermodynamic and geometric framework.
