# Vorticity dynamics

## Overview
The vorticity dynamics is a generalized vorticity equation that describes the evolution of the norm |Wg|∞ as a function of time and frequency. The equation is derived from the original vorticity equation for wave action conservation, but with a generalized weighting parameter g. The vorticity dynamics is used to study the asymptotic behavior of the norm |Wg|∞ as time approaches infinity.
