# Hoêva-Lifshitz Gravity

## Overview
Hoêva-Lifshitz gravity is a theoretical framework that describes the behavior of gravity at high energies and densities. This paper uses Hoêva-Lifshitz gravity to model dark energy and its relation to the universe's evolution.
