# Lateral Split-Gate p-i-n Junction

## Overview
Introduces an electrostatically defined junction for confining electron-hole overlap to a designed intrinsic in-plane recombination zone, enabling deterministic and electrically driven single-photon emission from localized defects in monolayer TMDs.
