# Traveling wave solutions

## Overview
The paper introduces traveling wave solutions to the vorticity dynamics, which describe the convergence towards a self-similar profile as time approaches infinity. The solutions are found for different ranges of g and describe the asymptotic behavior of the norm |Wg|∞ as time approaches infinity.
