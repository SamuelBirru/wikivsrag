# Back-to-back correlation

## Overview
A back-to-back correlation refers to a phenomenon where two particles are found in the opposite direction of each other, often observed in relativistic heavy-ion collisions. The squeezing effect can lead to an increase in back-to-back correlations due to in-medium mass modifications.
